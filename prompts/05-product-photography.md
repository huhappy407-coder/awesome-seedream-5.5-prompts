### 5.1 Prism serum bottle

<!-- ![Prism serum bottle](../images/product-photography/prism-serum-bottle.webp) -->

**Prompt:**

```text
Photograph a frosted cylindrical serum bottle on a chalk limestone block. Set the bottle just right of center with a blank cream label turned toward the lens. Send a narrow beam through a prism behind the set so a restrained spectrum crosses the wall while the bottle stays neutral. A large overhead silk describes the frosted shoulder; black flags define its edges. Luxury beauty editorial, ample copy space at upper left. Frame at 4:5.
```

- Aspect ratio: 4:5 | Use case: Skincare campaign hero | Industry: Beauty, E-commerce

### 5.2 White sweep headphones

<!-- ![White sweep headphones](../images/product-photography/white-sweep-headphones.webp) -->

**Prompt:**

```text
Photograph a single pair of charcoal over-ear headphones standing upright against a pure white seamless sweep. Rotate the near ear cup slightly to reveal the cushion and hinge; show the entire headband inside an 8% border. Two vertical softboxes trace the matte shells, with a tiny grounding shadow below the pads. Use crisp catalog focus, neutral white balance and accurate proportions. Keep props and type out of frame. Frame at 1:1.
```

- Aspect ratio: 1:1 | Use case: Marketplace main image | Industry: E-commerce

### 5.3 Citrus tonic pour

<!-- ![Citrus tonic pour](../images/product-photography/citrus-tonic-pour.webp) -->

**Prompt:**

```text
Freeze the moment a clear tonic stream enters a tall chilled glass on a tangerine counter. Set the glass on the left third with one long peel inside and a fan of bubbles under the surface. Backlight through diffusion to separate the stream; use a black card on the right to draw its edge. Low three-quarter camera, sharp droplets, natural condensation, clear upper right space for menu copy. Frame at 4:5.
```

- Aspect ratio: 4:5 | Use case: Seasonal drinks ad | Industry: Food & beverage

### 5.4 Desk kit flat lay

<!-- ![Desk kit flat lay](../images/product-photography/desk-kit-flat-lay.webp) -->

**Prompt:**

```text
Photograph a compact keyboard, braided cable, mouse and folded felt mat directly overhead on a light ash desk. Arrange exactly four objects in a loose rectangular grid, with the cable coiled in its own quadrant and all keys blank. A window to the left makes short parallel shadows. Use tidy editorial styling, neutral grays and a single rust accent; keep every item entirely in frame. Frame at 1:1.
```

- Aspect ratio: 1:1 | Use case: Bundle contents image | Industry: SaaS, E-commerce

### 5.5 Dew on cleansing balm

<!-- ![Dew on cleansing balm](../images/product-photography/dew-on-cleansing-balm.webp) -->

**Prompt:**

```text
Make a macro still life of an open mint-green cleansing balm jar on damp slate. The lid rests behind the jar; one smooth scoop in the balm reveals a waxy translucent edge. Set a low strip light behind the jar and a white card near camera so the rim stays round and readable. Use close commercial focus on the product surface with progressively softer droplets behind. Frame at 3:2.
```

- Aspect ratio: 3:2 | Use case: Beauty texture detail | Industry: Beauty

### 5.6 Pocket projector at dusk

<!-- ![Pocket projector at dusk](../images/product-photography/pocket-projector-at-dusk.webp) -->

**Prompt:**

```text
Place a palm-sized projector on a low oak shelf at dusk, pointing toward an off-white wall showing soft abstract color fields. Frame the device in the near right foreground and the projection on the left. Let the image cast a faint cool reflection on the perforated case; a warm practical lamp defines the focus dial. Maintain plausible lens alignment and a sharp grille in an understated home-tech photograph. Frame at 16:9.
```

- Aspect ratio: 16:9 | Use case: Electronics lifestyle shot | Industry: E-commerce, Gaming

### 5.7 Hammered silver hoop

<!-- ![Hammered silver hoop](../images/product-photography/hammered-silver-hoop.webp) -->

**Prompt:**

```text
Photograph one hammered silver hoop earring balanced on the edge of an unglazed porcelain tile. Turn the hinge toward camera so a buyer can inspect the closure. Use a long diffused strip reflected in the metal and two thin black flags to separate the polished edge from the pale backdrop. Square macro composition with the hinge on the upper third, realistic tool marks and soft depth falloff. Frame at 1:1.
```

- Aspect ratio: 1:1 | Use case: Jewelry detail page | Industry: E-commerce

### 5.8 Breakfast linen service

<!-- ![Breakfast linen service](../images/product-photography/breakfast-linen-service.webp) -->

**Prompt:**

```text
Stage a single oatmeal linen napkin folded loosely next to a stoneware plate on a small guesthouse breakfast table. Let a narrow band of dawn light cross the weave, with a blurred orchard beyond the window. Shoot at seated eye height; place the folded edge in the front left and the plate farther back to reveal scale. Warm hospitality photography, subtle wrinkles and room on the right for brochure text. Frame at 3:2.
```

- Aspect ratio: 3:2 | Use case: Hospitality scene image | Industry: Food & beverage, Real estate
